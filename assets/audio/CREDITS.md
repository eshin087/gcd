# Audio credits

Every recorded sound and piece of music in ABYSS comes from [OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org)
and is used under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication): no attribution is required, but these artists
made the game sound the way it does, so they are named here. Some pages offer more than one licence;
ABYSS uses the CC0 one. The files in `assets/audio/` were cut, layered, pitched and normalised from these
sources by `scripts/audio/build-samples.py` (ADR-117).

"Posted by" is the OpenGameArt account that published the page. Where the page says the sounds were made
by someone else, they were: Juhani Junkala (Boss Battle Music, via SubspaceAudio), StillNorthMedia
(Medieval sound effects, via MedicineStorm), Jan Schupke (Tinysized SFX, Fantasy Weapons and Apparel, via
Vehicle), independent.nu Ljudbank (37 hits/punches, 35 wooden cracks, via qubodup), Julien Matthey
(Fireball, via diligentcircle), Fantozzi (footsteps, via qubodup), dave.des (Large Wings Flap, via
AntumDeluge), and yd (Dark Shrine Loop is qubodup's remix of yd's Shrine).

Checked while picking: a pack whose bundled licence file contradicted its CC0 listing ("Energy Drain",
CC BY-SA inside) and recordings whose CC0 status could not be traced to their origin ("Gorilla Sounds")
were left out.
